## Features

* Sales Forecasting
* Business Analytics Dashboard
* CSV Upload
* Forecast Graphs
* AI Predictions

* Scikit-learn

## Forecast Explanation

The sales forecast predicts future sales trends based on historical business data. By analyzing past sales patterns, the model estimates expected sales for upcoming periods and helps identify potential growth or decline in demand.

## Business Value

This forecast can support business decision-making in several ways:

* Inventory Planning: Helps businesses maintain optimal stock levels and avoid overstocking or stock shortages.
* Demand Management: Enables better preparation for seasonal increases or decreases in customer demand.
* Financial Planning: Assists in budgeting, revenue estimation, and cash flow management.
* Resource Allocation: Helps managers plan staffing and operational resources more effectively.
* Strategic Decision-Making: Provides data-driven insights that support business growth and operational efficiency.

The forecasting dashboard transforms historical sales data into actionable business insights, helping store owners, startup founders, and business managers make informed planning decisions.

## Business Scenario

This project was built for a retail business that wants to predict future sales using historical sales data.

The forecasting system helps store managers and business owners analyze sales trends, estimate future demand, and make better decisions regarding inventory management, staffing, budgeting, and business planning.

By forecasting future sales, the business can reduce stock shortages, avoid overstocking, and improve overall operational efficiency.
